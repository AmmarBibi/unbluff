## METHOD

14 agents, 0 errors, 0 empty results, 2.12M subagent tokens, 46 min.

1. **Extract** - 6 agents in parallel, one per source slice (README split at line 150 so no
   agent skims). Each returned claims plus *its own reject count*: how many candidate
   sentences it examined and ruled non-behavioural. Those counts are below; a low one would
   mean an extractor was not really filtering.
2. **Refute** - pipelined, no barrier. Every PROVEN credit was attacked by a separate agent
   instructed to demote on doubt and to construct the counterexample explicitly.
3. **Critique** - 2 agents re-read the sources cold and hunted for claims the extractors
   never thought to look for, plus entries that are not really claims.
4. **Count** - N/M/K computed in the workflow script and in `build_matrix.py`, not asserted
   by any model.
